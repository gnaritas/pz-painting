Privacy

Privacy in plain English

This site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. Your browser contacts this site and, if you open the map or a link to Facebook, the service you requested — nothing else runs in the background.

What we collect

If you send the quote form, the details you type (name, phone, email and your message) are delivered to PZ Painting & Contracting, LLC’s inbox so the shop can answer your quote request. Nothing else about your visit is stored by the site itself.

Third-party links
